✦ Our Story

It Started With A Simple Question.

How can we make caring for a pet feel less complicated and more connected?

From everyday wellness and helpful resources to professional connections and moments that matter, FurEver Care was created to bring the pieces of pet care together in one thoughtful experience.

We believe technology should make care feel more human — not more complicated.

About FurEver Care

FurEver Care is a modern pet-care platform designed to connect pet owners with useful resources, services and a caring community.

The platform is designed for pet owners, veterinarians, shelters, rescue volunteers and people who care deeply about animals.

We focus on bringing helpful pet-care experiences together while keeping the technology simple, warm and easy to use.

Start by exploring FurEver Care and selecting the experience that best matches how you want to participate.
