✦ Quick First Aid

Stay Calm. Stay Safe.

Basic steps to keep in mind while contacting or traveling to a veterinarian.

01

Keep Your Pet Calm

Reduce noise and unnecessary movement. Approach frightened or injured animals slowly and carefully.

Keep surroundings quiet
Avoid unnecessary handling
Keep your pet comfortably warm
02

Check Basic Signs

Observe your pet without putting yourself or the animal at additional risk.

Observe breathing
Look for visible bleeding
Note changes in responsiveness
03

Contact Professional Help

Emergency situations require professional assessment. Contact an appropriate veterinary resource.

Call ahead when possible
Follow professional instructions
Transport your pet safely

Your Pet's Safety Comes First.

When something feels seriously wrong, trust your instincts and seek professional veterinary care. FurEver Care helps you find the right next step faster.

🚨 Immediate danger? Contact local emergency services.

Emergency Questions

Serious breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, collapse, seizures, suspected poisoning and other life-threatening symptoms require immediate professional attention.

When practical, calling ahead can help the veterinary team prepare for your arrival and give you instructions while you travel.

Home first aid should not replace professional veterinary assessment when your pet may have a serious or life-threatening condition.

Bring relevant medication information, your pet's medical details, and any safe packaging or information related to a suspected exposure.
